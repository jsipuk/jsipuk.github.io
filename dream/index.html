When the Night Learned to Swim

A story in five lights

The morning that went missing

The sun did not rise on Tuesday. By midday, the coast was still dark and the tide had climbed the lighthouse steps. The keeper rang the harbour bell until her hands hurt. No boat answered.

On the lantern glass, someone had written in salt: Morning has lost its way. Give it five lights to follow.

Above the black water, five small lights waited. The keeper had no map for what came next.
