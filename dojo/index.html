Settings and data

Security

This page is public, but the data never is: it lives in this browser only, on this device, and no version of it is ever sent anywhere or committed to the site. Encryption protects it from someone opening this page on your unlocked machine. It cannot protect you from someone who has both the device and the passphrase, and a forgotten passphrase means the data is gone.

Auto-lock

Backup

Browsers clear storage, and devices get replaced. An encrypted backup is safe to keep in Google Drive or anywhere else, because it is useless without your passphrase. Import merges rather than overwrites, so you will not lose reps made since the backup.

Erase

Clears only The Dojo's own storage key. Nothing else in this browser is touched.
