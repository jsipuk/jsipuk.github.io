# Riverside • the first shift

## A working block

Use the [route fixture](../fixtures/riverside-first-job.json) for IDs, positions and connected roads. At 72 × 48 tiles of 16 px, Riverside is deliberately small. Draw an orthogonal overhead map. Roads are roughly five tiles wide. Allow two vehicles to pass. The route skeleton can move after the handling test if the same connections remain.

The depot is southwest. Drive north and east to the ticket office. Take the long road round the builders’ yard or cut through its shutter. Both routes meet before the road splits between the ferry on the left and the market on the right. A wide lay-by lets you see the ticket-machine animation and park before the junction. On the first lap a camera sightline should reveal the shutter from the main road. Footpaths also let a player reach either recipient. A short route must save a perceptible turn or stretch of driving.

## Q01 • One good battery

**Target:** a direct run in 3–5 minutes from a new save, including reading and parking. An optional first encounter can take the curious player to 5–8 minutes. Resumed quick calls should fit five. Avoid a compulsory tutorial or countdown.

| Event | Player sees or does | Result and checkpoint |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Spawn | The van is parked outside the depot. A phone rings on the pavement. One prompt reads “Answer”. | Walking and ignoring the phone are both valid. |
| Accept | Mo: “Starter’s dead. Last ferry’s full. Tell me you’ve still got that battery.” | One battery enters the player's custody. Job = carrying. The quay is marked. |
| Counteroffer | Nell radios: “If that’s the spare, have a look up the high street. Every stall’s dark.” | Mark the market as a second destination. Fire this when leaving the depot on foot or by car. Persist that it was heard. |
| Find | At the ticket office, coins move uphill towards the return slot. A bottle cap scuttles away. | Optional inspection begins on foot. Leave or resume at the same settled turn. |
| Recruit | Raised claws → Dim → unclenched claws → Offer washer. | Add Clatterjack and its guide page exactly once. If full, send to depot roster. The starter journey has a free slot. |
| Lift | Select “Lift shutter” while standing beside it with Clatterjack active. | Play the animation, clear its collision and record yard access. It stays open in every later session. |
| Deliver | Walk up to Mo or Nell. Label the action “Give battery to Mo/Nell”. Name the other crew’s consequence on the confirmation. | Commit one outcome, both trust changes, the street change, cargo removal and the correct follow-up together. |
| Finish | Ferry starts, or stalls light up. Hear the first line of the recipient’s next call. | “Park up” and “Take the next call” are equally prominent. Returning shows the saved recipient. |

**Ferry ending.** Passengers board. Mo says, “That phone on the slipway’s ringing again. They pulled its cable out in ’98.” The market stays dark for this beat. **Market ending.** The stalls light up one at a time. Nell says, “Hear that under the floor? There hasn’t been a phone down there in years.” The ferry remains moored for this beat.

**Failure and free roam.** The van can always reach either end by the ring road. Ignoring the creature, choosing a different vehicle or getting caught leaves both deliveries possible. Borrowing the express car triggers the patrol once per new taking. Collecting a creature never consumes the only route through a case. On relaunch restore the latest settled action. A handover cannot move the same battery twice.

## The rest of shift one • The third ring

Q01 is the public demonstration of short play. The first longer shift uses the same saved outcome. Times are playtest targets for a first visit, not scripted delays.

| Beat | Approx. | Play | Visible result and stop point |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Q01 • One good battery | 3–5 min direct | Choose the recipient, or detour for a recruit. | A ferry runs or the market lights up. |
| Q02F • Low water / Q02M • Under the stalls | 4–6 min | Mo sends you down the slipway to follow wet footprints; Nell asks you to trace a bell under her stall. Each path reveals half a municipal warning label. | Journal gets the branch-specific rubbing and the old ticket-office address. |
| Q03 • Return to sender | 4–6 min | Find the matching label in the ticket office. Use Wickwing to reveal the faded number under the counter. If you missed Clatterjack, hear its coins on the way out. | A maintenance route and the depot switchboard are marked. |
| Q04 • Crossed lines | 5–8 min | Carry a replacement relay from the depot to the yard cabinet. Use the shutter if open or take the ring road. At the cabinet, match two large symbols shown in the journal. | The dead line rings. A previously dark warning lamp comes on. |
| Shift close • Three rings | 2–4 min | Follow the lamp to a riverside viewing point. Watch a line of pocket-sized creatures repeat the ring along the mud. Choose which crew hears your recording first. | Finish shift 1, log the sighting and open shift 2. The radio choice changes the reply and journal flavour. |

The four calls plus close target roughly 20–30 minutes including travel and curiosity. Each has its own checkpoint. Add one proper return journey, discovery or decision if a playtest feels thin; slow driving and unskippable dialogue are poor ways to fill time. Q02F and Q02M converge at Q03 so both paths can share later content.

## First-shift replay and side work

After Q01 the board in M2 always offers a short option. **Last post** is a depot-to-quay delivery of sorted mail with a best-time result. **Lost property** asks for one marked find from three authored sites; it records each site once. The board also points to the next story call. Only one job runs at a time. A story checkpoint can be parked between calls while a quick job is active. A short job has an explicit finish even if the player already knows the road.

On repeat, let quiet runs compete against quiet runs. An express-car run with heat belongs in the pursuit category even if heat clears before finishing. Record simulation time while the world is active. Backgrounding and menus pause scoring. Tracks stay available after completing the district.
