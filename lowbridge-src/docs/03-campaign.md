# A town worth coming back to

## The long game

Disconnected phones are ringing along an old flood-warning circuit. Each district reveals another piece. The sound turns out to be a chain of creatures mimicking the old test alarm as water moves through the culverts. In the final shift, people and companions give that signal a working route back through town. The battery decision stays in the log and shapes dialogue, contacts and approach routes.

The full campaign is a **content target after the first district passes playtests**. Three districts, nine authored shifts, three captain contests and nine companion species including the starter. Two recurring quick-job formats per district give six formats overall. The main route always has a solution with the starter and ordinary roads. Optional collection rewards curious players with different solutions.

**Pacing forecast.** Nine shifts averaging 25 minutes give about 3¾ hours of story. Optional sightings, mastery and route challenges might add 45–90 minutes, putting the design target near 4½–5¼ hours. At two 30-minute sessions plus three five-minute visits a week, that is roughly four weeks. These are design assumptions to measure; faster players can finish sooner. Quick sessions count towards the same campaign. No real-time gates or catch-up penalty.

**Gates.** Finish the three shifts in an area to enter the next. A mark is earned in the closing shift; a missing optional species or low crew trust never closes the main road. Each closing contest has a repeatable practice attempt. Open routes stay open after switching companions. The final operating choice changes the epilogue caller and dispatch location. All habitats remain reachable.

## What earns a return visit

- **One more short job.** Open the board and see a fixed-length option, the current story lead and one undiscovered clue. Keep options across days. Finishing a shift opens new authored sites.
- **A reason to drive back.** A new talent can do something at a recognisable old landmark. Example: take Reedling to the drained ticket hall and echo the test bell to find its missing guide observation.
- **A personal finish line.** Keep nine guide entries, three accomplishments per species, three district marks and persistent route bests. Offer these as distinct goals. The story remains finishable without completing the guide.
- **After the ending.** Return to both rival crews for their remaining favours, finish optional sightings or replay timed routes. A practice replay of a historic dispatch uses temporary state and never rewrites the campaign; defer this optional feature if save isolation is uncertain.

## Content budget and cut order

Per district, plan three 20–30 minute shifts, three species, one captain, two quick-job formats and one significant route change. Author the actual calls and tune their durations one district at a time. Keep both early branches local and converge on a common next clue. If capacity tightens, cut practice replays first, then cosmetic mastery variations, then extra side-job sites. Protect the first meaningful handover, at least one useful recruit, resumable shifts and a proper district ending.
