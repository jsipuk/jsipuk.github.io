    Keyboard
    • W A S D or arrows: walk, or drive with the pad preset (B: left/right steer, up is Go, down brakes)
    • Space: brake, then reverse
    • E or Enter: the action on the button (Get in, Get out, Answer, Hang up)
    • Esc or P: pause · 1/2: steering A/B · M: sound
