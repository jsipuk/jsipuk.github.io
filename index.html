I am a Consulting Sales Engineer at Netskope, with more than five years helping organisations turn complicated security and networking requirements into something practical. I specialise in SASE, SSE and Zero Trust, across enterprises of every size and most industries and regions.

How these are built

Everything here follows the same four rules. They are the reason the list holds together.

Your data stays with you

Nothing here has an account to create or a server to sign in to. What you type is stored in your own browser, on your own device.

No tracking

No analytics, no advertising, no third-party scripts. This page loads nothing from anywhere else, and neither do the apps.

Works without a signal

Most of these install to a home screen and run offline, which is the point when you are on a plane or in a basement.

Small and readable

Static files, no build step where one can be avoided, and source you can read in an afternoon. All of it is public on GitHub.

Personal

One of these is mine rather than yours. It is listed for my own convenience, and it will ask for a passphrase.
