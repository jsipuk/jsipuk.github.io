Research boundaries

This experiment makes no requests to Pokémon Center, Very, TotalCards, proxy services or payment providers. It does not prove that any named retailer has a vulnerability.

Key distinction: queue admission for a session and purchase eligibility for one real customer are different controls. This teaching model has no genuine person verification.
